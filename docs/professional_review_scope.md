# Professional Review Scope

This project supports technical preparation of reviewable records. It does not replace professional judgment.

## Reviewers Who May Be Needed

- CPAs or accounting professionals for financial reporting and accounting policy review.
- Tax preparers or tax advisors for Form 1099-DA, Form 8949, basis, and reporting review.
- Auditors for audit procedures, evidence sufficiency, and internal control review.
- Attorneys or compliance professionals for legal, regulatory, custody, money transmission, and jurisdictional questions.
- Security professionals for credential handling, private-key exposure, webhook validation, and production hardening.

## Items Outside Repository Claims

- Final tax return preparation or filing.
- Final information return preparation or filing.
- Legal conclusions about regulatory status.
- Audit opinions or attestations.
- Government approval or endorsement.
- Guaranteed compliance.
- Guaranteed adoption or deployment.

## Acceptable Output Language

Use phrases such as "support schedule," "readiness output," "mapping support," "reviewable evidence," "draft journal-entry preparation," and "professional review workflow."

Avoid phrases such as "IRS filing system," "tax compliance guaranteed," "auditor-approved," "government-approved," or "CPA replacement."
