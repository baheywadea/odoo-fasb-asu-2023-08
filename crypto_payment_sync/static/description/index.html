How it works

A simple, reliable flow for both website and back-office payments:

  1. Configure supported crypto currencies/networks and company wallets.
  2. Create a payment request (customer or vendor) and select currency + wallet.
  3. Pay & track: customer completes payment via WalletConnect/QR, or you record an on-chain tx hash.
  4. Validate and update status once confirmed on-chain.
  5. Post accounting entries automatically and keep a full audit trail.
Tip
Keep a dedicated journal for crypto payments (e.g., Crypto Payments) to simplify reporting and reconciliation.
